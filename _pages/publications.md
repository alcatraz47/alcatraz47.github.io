## Publication
**Data Mining Techniques to Categorise Single Paragraph-Formed Self-narrated Stories (Springer)**
In this work, we explored how to mine and categorize self-narrated paragraph stories into actionable themes. We designed a text-mining pipeline to separate narratives into categories such as work stress, bullying, and harassment (including cyber harassment), focusing on practical feature engineering and classification for real-world, noisy writing.

https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-15-8354-4_70

## Capstone
**Krishok — An IoT-Based Intelligent Farming Solution**
For our capstone, we built an end-to-end prototype combining mobile + IoT for practical farming support. The system enables rice disease detection from leaf images via an Android app, designed to work under constrained connectivity. We addressed limited data using manual collection and augmentation and validated the approach through iterative testing.

https://www.researchgate.net/publication/336639606_Krishok_-An_IoT_Based_Intelligent_Farming_Solution
